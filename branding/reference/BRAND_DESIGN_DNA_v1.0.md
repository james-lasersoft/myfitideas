# MyFitIdeas Brand Design DNA v1.0

## Brand premise
MyFitIdeas represents renewal, growth, transformation, and human potential. The identity should feel like the urgency of spring growth: energetic and optimistic, but never neon, childish, or visually exhausting.

## Design principles
1. Geometry over tracing.
2. Meaning over decoration.
3. Purposeful negative space.
4. Organic curves built with disciplined precision.
5. Human-centered proportions and readability.
6. Vibrant restraint.
7. Accessible hierarchy and redundant meaning.
8. Scalability from favicon to large-format display.

## Symbol meanings
- Top leaf: aspiration and renewal.
- Head: individual identity and human potential.
- Body: transformation and emergence.
- Left leaf: support and foundation.
- Right leaf: balance and sustained growth.
- Negative space: openness, movement, and continuous growth.

## Product translation
The design DNA applies to UI components, icons, charts, motion, illustrations, presentations, and marketing. The brand should support the data rather than compete with it.
