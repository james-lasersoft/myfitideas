# MyFitIdeas Typography Standard - Candidate v1.0

## Wordmark
- Canonical name treatment: `MyFitIdeas`
- Target typeface: Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold
- Status: Candidate direction selected; final production font file and outlined master pending
- Primary color candidate: Charcoal `#1F2937`

## Product typography
- Display and headings: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Body and interface: Inter
- Data: Inter with tabular numerals where supported

## Wordmark refinement rules
- Begin with optical kerning, especially `My`, `yF`, `Fi`, `tI`, `Id`, `de`, `ea`, and `as`.
- Keep customization subtle and legibility-first.
- Do not replace the dots of the lowercase i characters until small-size tests confirm clarity.
- Convert the final approved wordmark to outlines for canonical vector lockups.
- Preserve a licensed editable type version in the controlled source archive.

## Important production note
The current visual study uses a metrically similar installed proxy because Plus Jakarta Sans was not available in the generation environment. The final wordmark must be rebuilt with the licensed Plus Jakarta Sans font before approval.
