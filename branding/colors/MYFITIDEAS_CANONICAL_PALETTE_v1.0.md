# MyFitIdeas Canonical Palette v1.0

## Purpose

This document records the initial canonical color system for the MyFitIdeas brand identity. It is intended to support consistent use across the master logo, web application, future mobile applications, marketing materials, documentation, and preliminary trademark or logo-registration records.

The palette expresses early-spring growth, transformation, vitality, optimism, and renewal. These values are part of the visual foundation of the MyFitIdeas brand.

## Logo Rendering Standard

The canonical master logo should be maintained as flat vector artwork with solid fills and no drop shadows, bevels, texture effects, or mandatory gradients. A separate enhanced marketing or hero version may later use controlled gradients, but it must not replace the canonical flat master.

## Registration and Recordkeeping Notes

For logo-registration, trademark-support, and brand-governance records, retain the following together:

- The approved master SVG.
- The canonical color assignments in this document.
- A monochrome version.
- A reversed white version for dark backgrounds.
- Date and version of approval.
- First commercial-use date when known.
- Examples of use in the web application, mobile application, and marketing materials.

Color alone should not be the only defining characteristic of the mark. The geometry, arrangement, and negative-space relationships of the five design elements remain central to the identity.
